Do **not** upgrade the new details to `CONFIRMED` until a directly authenticated publisher/book excerpt or Disney statement is located. Disney's [confirmed three-universe existential-threat synopsis](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) remains the canonical baseline.

**CONFIRMED licensed partner campaign, original November 4 (archived November 5):** [Dave & Buster's official announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dave--busters-surrenders-to-doom-introducing-doom--busters-302928815.html) launches the temporary `Doom & Buster's` brand and a fuller **November 16–January 31** arcade takeover with themed games, prizes and collectible Power Cards. **Checked November 5:** [Disney still lists December 18, 2026 in U.S. theaters](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday); no confirmed release shift or new cinematic footage. [Sourced Nov. 5 checkpoint](../news/2026-11-05.md).

## November 3, 2026 — official Japanese Doctor Doom dubbing

**CONFIRMED November 3 original source:** [Marvel Japan's official announcement](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/movie/avengers-doomsday/news/20261103_01) names **Toshiyuki Morikawa (森川智之)** the **Japanese voice actor** for **Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom**. The accompanying [Japanese-dubbed trailer](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/avengersdoomsday_trailers_dubbing_hd_2398p_stereo_digital) and [Downey voice-casting message](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/avengersdoomsday_japandoomvoicetalentannounce_hd_2398p_stereo) are official regionally released promotion. Morikawa's casting is **localization**, not a change to the on-screen actor, a new U.S. cast member, or proof of a Tony Stark/Doctor Doom identity reveal. Downey's on-record video honors the late **Keiji Fujiwara**, who voiced his Tony Stark in Japan.

The same primary source features Japan's planned premium-format presentations—INFINITY VISION, Dolby Cinema, 4DX and SCREENX—with Doom-themed posters. Similar premium-format artwork was reported as early as **October 29**, so its November 3 **Japan press-page date is not proof of its first global release**. Localized trailer footage has **not** been verified as showing a new cinematic shot over the previously cataloged trailer; [video catalog](trailers-and-promos.md). [U.S. release remains December 18](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday). No *Secret Wars*, Battleworld or Thor confrontation outcome is established by these marketing descriptions.

His participation in the film was already publicly confirmed in **2025**; this is **not** a new casting announcement. ## Confirmed production and release facts

- **U.S. theatrical release currently scheduled for December 18, 2026.** [Disney Movies' official *Avengers: Doomsday* page](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday), checked through 2026-10-29.
- **Official studio synopsis:** heroes from **three distinct universes** are on a collision course facing an existential threat. That is official marketing language, **not** a named confirmation of an *incursion* or *Battleworld*. [Disney Movies](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday).
- **Directors:** Anthony and Joe Russo; **credited writer:** Stephen McFeely. [Disney Movies](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday).
- **Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom:** announced by Marvel/Disney, with Downey included in [Disney's official current film casting list](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday). See also [Disney's 2025 cast announcement coverage](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/avengers-doomsday-cast-livestream-views/).
- The official cast list is extensive and subject to future revision. Refer to the primary [Disney listing](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) for the currently public names rather than treating rumored cameos as confirmed.
