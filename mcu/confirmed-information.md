# Avengers: Doomsday — verified facts tracker

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-26. This page records information corroborated by primary studio/distributor/partner sources; it does **not** promote extrapolations from advertising into film canon.

## Confirmed production and release facts

- **U.S. theatrical release currently scheduled for December 18, 2026.** [Disney Movies' official *Avengers: Doomsday* page](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday), checked 2026-10-26.
- **Official studio synopsis:** heroes from **three distinct universes** are on a collision course facing an existential threat. That is official marketing language, **not** a named confirmation of an *incursion* or *Battleworld*. [Disney Movies](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday).
- **Directors:** Anthony and Joe Russo; **credited writer:** Stephen McFeely. [Disney Movies](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday).
- **Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom:** announced by Marvel/Disney, with Downey included in [Disney's official current film casting list](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday). See also [Disney's 2025 cast announcement coverage](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/avengers-doomsday-cast-livestream-views/).
- The official cast list is extensive and subject to future revision. Refer to the primary [Disney listing](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) for the currently public names rather than treating rumored cameos as confirmed.

## Official promotion is not automatically film canon

- MLB reports that [the October 23 World Series special preview](https://www.mlb.com/news/avengers-doomsday-game-1-of-the-2026-world-series) features the Fantastic Four's Reed Richards at Ebbets Field and an appearance by Doom over the ballpark. **MLB explicitly identifies its baseball stars as additions for the special advertisement**. Do not claim they appear in the movie on this basis.
- [October 25 Marvel marketing](https://x.com/Marvel/status/2114144910316298623) reinforces a team-up theme. Specific frame/timecode analysis remains under review in the [trailer tracker](trailers-and-promos.md).

## What is NOT confirmed here

- The exact cause of the collision of universes; an on-screen God Emperor Doom or Battleworld; the final outcome of any Fantastic Four or Avengers confrontation.
- That Jonathan Hickman holds a formal, credited consulting role on both *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars*. His [October 21, 2026 interview](https://bleedingcool.com/comics/lucca-jonathan-hickman-works-on-more-films-than-anyone-will-know/) establishes script-reading and development-stage exposure but not a credit.
- Any MLB athlete's canonical appearance in the theatrical movie.
