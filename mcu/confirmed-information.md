- MLB reports that [the October 23 World Series special preview](https://www.mlb.com/news/avengers-doomsday-game-1-of-the-2026-world-series) features the Fantastic Four's Reed Richards at Ebbets Field and an appearance by Doom over the ballpark. **MLB explicitly identifies its baseball stars as additions for the special advertisement**. Do not claim they appear in the movie on this basis.
