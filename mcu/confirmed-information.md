# Avengers: Doomsday — verified facts tracker

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-30. This page records information corroborated by primary studio/distributor/partner sources; it does **not** promote extrapolations from advertising into film canon.

## Professor X / Patrick Stewart — October 29–30, 2026

**CONFIRMED:** Marvel Studios mourned Sir Patrick Stewart after his **October 28 death** (publicly announced **October 29**) and identified *Avengers: Doomsday* as his **final onscreen appearance** playing Professor Charles Xavier. His participation in the film was already publicly confirmed in **2025**; this is **not** a new casting announcement. See [Variety's October 29–30 Marvel tribute coverage](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/global/patrick-stewart-death-marvel-kevin-feige-tributes-x-men-42099/) and [October 30 final-role confirmation](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/patrick-stewart-last-movie-role-avengers-doomsday-42112). His passing does **not** confirm Xavier's film fate, any script edit, reshoots, a studio dedication, or a Doom/Xavier plot connection. [October 30 research entry](../news/2026-10-30.md).

**Verified regional release update (2026-10-28):** [Marvel India’s official October 28 announcement](https://x.com/Marvel_India/status/2115315582094692356) gives **December 17, 2026** for **India**, with English, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu showings; [The Hollywood Reporter India (October 28)](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/features/insight/avengers-doomsday-set-for-early-india-release-robert-downey-jr-chris-evans-share-special-diwali-message-for-fans) corroborates the regional announcement. The **U.S. date remains December 18, 2026** on [Disney's official movie page](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday). This is a **regional** date announcement, not a U.S. or global date change. The accompanying Downey/Evans Diwali greeting is official marketing, not proof of Doom's comic origins or any new narrative sequence.

**Other distributor-confirmed regional dates (backfilled 2026-10-29):** [Disney UK's original July 27, 2026 Comic-Con release](https://press.disney.co.uk/news/marvel-studios-captivates-san-diego-comic-con-with-exclusive-footage,-surprise-appearances-and-major-announcements-during-showcase-led-by-kevin-feige) specifies a **December 16, 2026 UK cinema opening**; this is a **July announcement newly indexed**, not an October 29 change. [Marvel Japan's October 28 and 29 official pages](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/movie/avengers-doomsday/news/20261029_01) and its [film page](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/movie/avengers-doomsday) specify **December 18, 2026 for Japan**. Thus, the sourced dates in this archive are **UK Dec 16; India Dec 17; Japan and U.S. Dec 18**, subject to future distributor updates.

## Confirmed production and release facts

- **U.S. theatrical release currently scheduled for December 18, 2026.** [Disney Movies' official *Avengers: Doomsday* page](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday), checked through 2026-10-29.
- **Official studio synopsis:** heroes from **three distinct universes** are on a collision course facing an existential threat. That is official marketing language, **not** a named confirmation of an *incursion* or *Battleworld*. [Disney Movies](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday).
- **Directors:** Anthony and Joe Russo; **credited writer:** Stephen McFeely. [Disney Movies](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday).
- **Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom:** announced by Marvel/Disney, with Downey included in [Disney's official current film casting list](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday). See also [Disney's 2025 cast announcement coverage](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/avengers-doomsday-cast-livestream-views/).
- The official cast list is extensive and subject to future revision. Refer to the primary [Disney listing](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) for the currently public names rather than treating rumored cameos as confirmed.

## Official promotion is not automatically film canon

- MLB reports that [the October 23 World Series special preview](https://www.mlb.com/news/avengers-doomsday-game-1-of-the-2026-world-series) features the Fantastic Four's Reed Richards at Ebbets Field and an appearance by Doom over the ballpark. **MLB explicitly identifies its baseball stars as additions for the special advertisement**. Do not claim they appear in the movie on this basis.
- [October 25 Marvel marketing](https://x.com/Marvel/status/2114144910316298623) reinforces a team-up theme. Specific frame/timecode analysis remains under review in the [trailer tracker](trailers-and-promos.md).

## What is NOT confirmed here

- The exact cause of the collision of universes; an on-screen God Emperor Doom or Battleworld; the final outcome of any Fantastic Four or Avengers confrontation.
- That Jonathan Hickman holds a formal, credited consulting role on both *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars*. His [October 21, 2026 interview](https://bleedingcool.com/comics/lucca-jonathan-hickman-works-on-more-films-than-anyone-will-know/) establishes script-reading and development-stage exposure but not a credit.
- Any MLB athlete's canonical appearance in the theatrical movie.
