## Verified MLB sequence — reference observations

**Source:** MLB, 2026-10-24, [official article](https://www.mlb.com/news/avengers-doomsday-game-1-of-the-2026-world-series).

- **Confirmed by publisher:** Reed Richards performs a ceremonial first pitch at Ebbets Field in the Fantastic Four's world; the footage uses his stretching ability. Doctor Doom appears over the ballpark near the end of the advertisement.
- **Important editing distinction:** MLB players Freddie Freeman, Randy Arozarena, Jacob Misiorowski, Bryce Harper and Junior Caminero were added to the stadium footage **for the promotional piece**. Do **not** treat these cameos as established movie canon.
- **Comic parallel (THEORY):** Reed and Doom's conflict is foundational to *Fantastic Four* history, including *Fantastic Four* #5 (1962). This is contextual research, not proof of a particular movie storyline.
