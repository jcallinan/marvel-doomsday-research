# Theory ledger: Doctor Doom and Scarlet Witch in *Avengers: Doomsday*

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-11. **Spoilers for the *VisionQuest* season finale.** Every forward-looking claim is **THEORY**, even when anchored to a confirmed MCU scene.

## Narrow claim already supported

**ON-SCREEN MCU SETUP, corroborated by independent reports:** The *VisionQuest* finale's secret post-credits scene shows Downey's Doctor Doom removing Mount Wundagore rubble from Elizabeth Olsen's Wanda Maximoff; Wanda awakens and Doom expresses his desire for her power. This is a direct MCU narrative connection, **not** yet a standalone *Doomsday* movie scene. The sequence was reportedly withheld from pre-release press screeners. [EW, original Nov. 10 11:01 p.m. ET](https://ew.com/visionquest-avengers-doomsday-post-credits-scene-elizabeth-olsen-scarlet-witch-return-12248438); [People, Nov. 11](https://people.com/visionquest-finale-tease-this-unexpected-character-return-surprise-end-credit-scene-12248528); [Disney+ official series page](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-8744ec55-8d14-4631-99b4-c889a2d6013f).

## Comic foundation

Allan Heinberg/Jim Cheung's ***Avengers: The Children's Crusade* (2010–2012)** involves Billy and Tommy's search for Wanda, Doom's role in **Latveria**, and an attempt to gain her reality-warping magic. This is unusually specific precedent, supported by [Marvel's Young Avengers history](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/young-avengers), [*The Children's Crusade* #3](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/31138/avengers-the-childrens-crusade-2010-3) and [#5](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/31142/read). See [the comics dossier](../comics/storylines/avengers-the-childrens-crusade-2010.md).

## Testable hypotheses

|---|---|---|---|
| Doom **seeks access to** Wanda's magic | Explicit motivation in the televised post-credits scene | He has **not been shown succeeding** | **Directly supported as a goal**, not accomplished transfer |
| *Doomsday* uses some *Children's Crusade* plot elements | Doom/Wanda/boys and desire for power align with comics | Film could resolve the arc off-screen or adapt a different story | **Moderate** as a thematic influence; **unconfirmed** as plot-by-plot adaptation |
| Wanda appears onscreen in *Avengers: Doomsday* | The series tees up the film immediately before release | [Disney's film cast list](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) does **not list Elizabeth Olsen** at this check | **Strong implication**, **not officially film-cast confirmed** |

| Wanda is one of the masked Latverian witches | Promotional witches plus Wanda association | No original casting or on-screen identification | **Rumor/theory only; low confidence** |
| Doom uses her magic to form Battleworld or control incursions | Reality-altering power + *Secret Wars* precedent | No film footage or official synopsis naming this mechanism | **Speculative; low confidence** |
| Doom and Wanda reproduce a comic betrothal/romance | Comic relationship in *Children's Crusade* | Nothing in the shown scene implies romance or agreement | **Not supported by MCU scene** |

## What would update these assessments?

1. An **official film cast addition** or directly shown *Doomsday* trailer footage with Olsen would establish her film appearance.
2. A **released movie scene or first-party synopsis** showing transfer/use of Wanda's powers would establish mechanics and an achieved outcome.
3. A **named original source** identifying a Latverian witch as Wanda would substantiate that character ID.
4. Explicit **Battleworld/incursion dialogue or film scenes** would materially connect this *Children's Crusade*-style thread with the 2015 *Secret Wars* arc.

**Alternative interpretations:** Doom could try and fail to exploit Wanda, Wanda could defeat him, or the scene could set up a later Marvel project. A scene following Wanda's apparent death does not alone resolve her later agency, how long she was incapacitated, or whether Doom brought her back to life versus simply awakened a survivor. **No Tony Stark secret-identity inference** follows from the same actor playing Doom.
