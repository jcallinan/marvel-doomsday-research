# Theory ledger: Reed Richards and Victor von Doom — intellectual rivals and former allies

**Created / archive review:** November 14, 2026. **Interview news originally published:** November 13, 2026. **Status:** The actors' depiction of a shared intellectual bond is **ON RECORD**; specific *Doomsday* flashbacks, institutions, betrayal details and outcomes are **UNCONFIRMED**.

## Movie evidence

- **ON-RECORD Pedro Pascal, November 13 Empire reporting:** Reed Richards and Victor von Doom think together at a level of science and mathematics few others match. Pascal characterizes a **friendship/kinship** alongside intellectual equality. [Nerdist's November 13 report](https://nerdist.com/article/avengers-doomsday-pedro-pascal-on-reed-doom-science-bro-relationship/); [ComicBookMovie's November 13 coverage](https://comicbookmovie.com/avengers/avengers-doomsday/avengers-doomsday-star-pedro-pascal-reveals-mister-fantastics-unique-bond-with-doctor-doom-a230983). Direct access to Empire's paywalled full article was unavailable; attribution is corroborated.
- **ON-RECORD Feige, November 12 (previous checkpoint):** Doctor Doom is **Victor von Doom** rather than a Tony Stark variant. [Previously archived Feige evidence](../news/2026-11-13.md). Sharing an actor with Iron Man is **not** evidence Doom is Reed's MCU version of Tony.
- **OFFICIAL movie premise:** [Disney's U.S. movie description](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) says heroes of three universes collide; it does not identify a university or explain why Reed and Doom parted ways.

## Comic precedents, separated from film canon

- **Publisher-confirmed baseline:** Marvel's [Fantastic Four comics history](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/fantastic-four/in-comics) identifies Doom as a **college rival** of Reed and a recurring F4 enemy. College rivalry is **not proof** that every canon version was a warm friendship.
- **Modern character study:** Marvel's [*Challenges of Doom: Mr. Fantastic* #1 publisher listing](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/135609/challenges_of_doom_mr_fantastic_2026_1) (July 2026; Al Ewing and Mike Henderson, per the [existing comics research](../comics/storylines/challenges-of-doom-2026.md)) explores young Victor/Reed competition. Comics publication is independent of an adaptation commitment.
- **Multiversal background:** Hickman's *Fantastic Four*, *New Avengers*, and *Secret Wars* stories use complex Reed/Doom ideological opposition, but none of those plots has been announced as the exact *Doomsday* script.

Distinguish romance of former-friends plot structure from the **comic's recurring distrust and rivalry**.
