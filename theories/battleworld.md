# Theory: Battleworld

## November 16 — Secret Wars interview describes a different world, not a named Battleworld

**ON-RECORD AS ATTRIBUTED, original November 16:** [The Playlist's report of Feige and Joe Russo's *Empire* comments](https://theplaylist.net/kevin-feige-joe-russo-avengers-secret-wars-20261116/) says *Secret Wars* is meant to be very different from *Doomsday* and invokes an unusually conceived world. The full original interview was not accessed; these comments are **about the 2027 sequel**, not a confirmed setting in the December 2026 movie.

**Interpretation:** A distinct world could evoke either the Beyonder's Battleworld in the 1984–85 comic or Doctor Doom's surviving-domain Battleworld in the 2015 comic ([background to the two separate events](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/features/marvel-secret-wars-explained-avengers-event-mcu/)). Neither Feige nor Russo in this cited passage explicitly names Battleworld, Doom's reign or the 2015 comics' universe patchwork. **Status remains THEORY; no confidence increase**. See [Nov. 16 news ledger](../news/2026-11-16.md) and [Secret Wars comparison](secret-wars-connections.md).

## November 15 — Doom's November 13 Disney+ promo dialogue does not establish Battleworld

[Marvel Entertainment's original November 13 “Every Moment” promo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kl7nG1nD0mk), newly cataloged during the November 15 review, reportedly ends with a Doom demand for obedience ([Nov. 14 account](https://www.9to5marvel.com/doctor-doom-new-line-avengers-doomsday-spot/)). **Interpretation:** the rhetoric is compatible with Doom's authoritarian monarch and *Secret Wars* (2015) God Emperor portrayal; **it is not** first-party evidence that the film depicts a patchwork Battleworld, Doom's power source or a specific comics adaptation. Original promo date **November 13**, archive check **November 15**; no increase from LOW–MODERATE theory confidence. [Dated source audit](../news/2026-11-15.md).

THEORY. FOR: Doom rules Battleworld in Secret Wars (2015), and the upcoming Avengers films evoke that era. AGAINST: no official confirmation that the film depicts the comics version of Battleworld. Confidence: LOW–MODERATE.

## November 6, 2026 — Jeep/Marvel partner video: no confidence increase

[Jeep/Stellantis's November 6 primary press release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jeep-brand-takes-adventure-to-marvel-studios-avengers-doomsday-universe-in-epic-finale-of-sweeping-yearlong-global-collaboration-302931380.html) says its [co-branded film-related commercial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uZjSPSuYY7c) travels across iconic universes and explicitly visits **Latveria**. This is **CONFIRMED advertising geography**, not proof the theatrical plot combines those worlds into the composite realm **Battleworld** from Jonathan Hickman's `Secret Wars` (2015). Ads are free to juxtapose settings, portals and characters without depicting screenplay events. **Officially named `Doomstadt Tower` and battleworld-style portal mechanisms have not been verified**; Reddit/fan speculation and secondary screenshots cannot fill that gap. **THEORY confidence remains LOW–MODERATE**, pending an explicit original film source.
