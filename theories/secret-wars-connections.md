# Theory: *Secret Wars* connections

**Classification: THEORY. Last reviewed 2026-10-26.** The 1984–85 *Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* and the 2015–16 *Secret Wars* are different stories. The MCU could borrow elements from either, both, or neither.

## Comic-canon comparisons

- **1984–85:** Doom's pursuit of near-cosmic power is a major part of *Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* #1–12. This is one precedent for Doom confronting a large crossover cast.
- **2015–16:** Jonathan Hickman's *Avengers* and *New Avengers* runs explore reality-threatening incursions, leading into *Secret Wars* (2015), where Doom rules Battleworld.
- **Character foundation:** Reed Richards and Doom are long-standing antagonists, going back to *Fantastic Four* #5 (1962). [Reading-order guide](../comics/reading-order.md).

## New film evidence reviewed 2026-10-26

- **Confirmed:** [Disney's official synopsis](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) describes heroes from **three distinct universes** on a collision course, but does not name incursions or Battleworld.
- **Confirmed official promotion:** [MLB's report dated 2026-10-24](https://www.mlb.com/news/avengers-doomsday-game-1-of-the-2026-world-series) describes Doom appearing over an Ebbets Field scene in the Fantastic Four's world. The MLB players are promo-specific edits, not confirmed film characters.
- **Reported interview:** In [Bleeding Cool's 2026-10-21 interview](https://bleedingcool.com/comics/lucca-jonathan-hickman-works-on-more-films-than-anyone-will-know/), Hickman says he has read changing *Doomsday* scripts and viewed unfinished scenes, but has not seen the final cut. The interview **does not confirm** a scene-by-scene adaptation, that Hickman determined the plot, or a formal credited role on both movies.

## Competing interpretations

**For a 2015-*Secret Wars* influence:** Multiple universes collide in the announced premise; Reed and Doom are both present; Hickman's comic saga is a pertinent comparison; Hickman has some development-stage visibility.

**Against a literal adaptation:** The trailer marketing and interview do not identify Battleworld, a God Emperor persona, or the precise incursion mechanics. A three-universe premise is compatible with many original plots. Prior superhero films have changed comics extensively.

**Assessment:** A *Secret Wars*-inspired multiversal conflict is a reasonable **theory**, **not confirmed film canon**. This page does not assign numerical odds. Watch for an explicit in-film or official explanation of how the realities interact.
