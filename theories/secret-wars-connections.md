# Theory: *Secret Wars* connections

## November 16 evidence update — Feige and Joe Russo describe a deliberately different sequel

**ORIGINAL REPORT: November 16, 2026; ARCHIVE CHECK: November 16.** [The Playlist's contemporaneous *Empire*-attributed interview report](https://theplaylist.net/kevin-feige-joe-russo-avengers-secret-wars-20261116/) quotes **Kevin Feige** calling *Avengers: Secret Wars* "a whole other genre of story" than *Avengers: Doomsday*. **Joe Russo** describes an unusual world and ambitions to show audiences something unfamiliar. **ON-RECORD AS REPORTED**: the named filmmaker remarks are attributable to *Empire*, but the complete original magazine interview was not independently reviewed. [Dark Horizons' same-day write-up](https://www.darkhorizons.com/avengers-secret-wars-switches-genres/) is corroborating reportage, not separate film-source confirmation.

**EVIDENCE HIERARCHY:**
- **Confirmed earlier studio material, not newly discovered November film footage:** [Disney's September 29 original *Endgame: Encore* feature](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/russo-brothers-avengers-endgame-doomsday/) already describes **incursions**, reinforced by Feige's **November 13** movie comments ([November 15 correction](incursions.md)). The historical October 26 snapshot below, which relies on a synopsis not explicitly naming incursions, is superseded on this specific point.
- **Supported filmmaker intention:** *Secret Wars* is intended to differ in form/tone/setting from *Doomsday*. The comments are chiefly **about the 2027 sequel**, not an authenticated *Doomsday* ending.
- **Not supported as a disclosed film event:** a confirmed Battleworld, a Beyonder appearance, a Doom victory, God Emperor Doom, patched-together worlds, or the comics' exact reality-collapse mechanism.

**COMICS COMPARISON, NOT ADAPTATION CONFIRMATION:** [*Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* (1984–85)](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/features/marvel-secret-wars-explained-avengers-event-mcu/) features the Beyonder and a constructed Battleworld, whereas Jonathan Hickman's [*Secret Wars* (2015)](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/features/marvel-secret-wars-explained-avengers-event-mcu/) explores a surviving composite world after incursions ruled by Doom. The suggested "unique world" is **compatible with** either broad precedent and many original stories, but **does not name either on-screen interpretation**. Keep Battleworld at **LOW–MODERATE hypothesis**, not confirmed; see [Battleworld ledger](battleworld.md) and [Nov. 16 research checkpoint](../news/2026-11-16.md).

**Classification: THEORY. Last reviewed 2026-10-26.** The 1984–85 *Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* and the 2015–16 *Secret Wars* are different stories. The MCU could borrow elements from either, both, or neither.

## Comic-canon comparisons

- **1984–85:** Doom's pursuit of near-cosmic power is a major part of *Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* #1–12. This is one precedent for Doom confronting a large crossover cast.
- **2015–16:** Jonathan Hickman's *Avengers* and *New Avengers* runs explore reality-threatening incursions, leading into *Secret Wars* (2015), where Doom rules Battleworld.
- **Character foundation:** Reed Richards and Doom are long-standing antagonists, going back to *Fantastic Four* #5 (1962). [Reading-order guide](../comics/reading-order.md).

## New film evidence reviewed 2026-10-26

- **Confirmed:** [Disney's official synopsis](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) describes heroes from **three distinct universes** on a collision course, but does not name incursions or Battleworld.
- **Confirmed official promotion:** [MLB's report dated 2026-10-24](https://www.mlb.com/news/avengers-doomsday-game-1-of-the-2026-world-series) describes Doom appearing over an Ebbets Field scene in the Fantastic Four's world. The MLB players are promo-specific edits, not confirmed film characters.
- **Reported interview:** In [Bleeding Cool's 2026-10-21 interview](https://bleedingcool.com/comics/lucca-jonathan-hickman-works-on-more-films-than-anyone-will-know/), Hickman says he has read changing *Doomsday* scripts and viewed unfinished scenes, but has not seen the final cut. The interview **does not confirm** a scene-by-scene adaptation, that Hickman determined the plot, or a formal credited role on both movies.

On **November 5**, [Marvel Studios announced a nine-city promotional tour](https://x.com/MarvelStudios/status/2118402984933831065) (corroborated by [the November 5 Las Vegas Review-Journal report](https://neon.reviewjournal.com/movies/marvel-bringing-its-doomsday-global-tour-to-las-vegas-3345436/)), using Doctor Doom art and a countdown. On **November 6 at 10:00 ET**, [Stellantis/Jeep formally introduced a partner commercial](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jeep-brand-takes-adventure-to-marvel-studios-avengers-doomsday-universe-in-epic-finale-of-sweeping-yearlong-global-collaboration-302931380.html) ([company-linked video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uZjSPSuYY7c)) visiting the Avengers, X-Men and Fantastic Four's fictional settings and **Latveria**. **Confirmed real-world promotion** does not verify that these settings merge in the actual film, that `Secret Wars` (2015) is adapted scene-for-scene, that an incursion creates Battleworld, or that Doom becomes God Emperor. No theory confidence increased. [Source-and-date note](../news/2026-11-06.md).

## Competing interpretations

**For a 2015-*Secret Wars* influence:** Multiple universes collide in the announced premise; Reed and Doom are both present; Hickman's comic saga is a pertinent comparison; Hickman has some development-stage visibility.

**Against a literal adaptation:** The trailer marketing and interview do not identify Battleworld, a God Emperor persona, or the precise incursion mechanics. A three-universe premise is compatible with many original plots. Prior superhero films have changed comics extensively.

**Assessment:** A *Secret Wars*-inspired multiversal conflict is a reasonable **theory**, **not confirmed film canon**. This page does not assign numerical odds. Watch for an explicit in-film or official explanation of how the realities interact.
