# Source and classification policy

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | Primary Marvel/Disney/official distributor statement or official published footage |
| Reported | Credible journalist or outlet with attributable reporting; not independently confirmed |
| Rumor | Unverified leak, anonymous claim, or uncertain secondary source |
| Theory | Original inference or speculation based on explicitly listed evidence |
| Comic canon | What a specified comic depicts; **not** a film confirmation |

Prefer official Marvel issue listings, comic indicia, credited interviews, Disney announcements, and canonical video uploads. Link sources with publication and access dates. Correct or archive outdated claims instead of silently overwriting history. Avoid unsupported confidence percentages.
