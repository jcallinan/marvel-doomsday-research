## November 24 — new on-record cast and filmmaker cover story

**ON RECORD / REPUTABLE ORIGINAL REPORTING:** [Nick Romano, Entertainment Weekly, November 24, 2026, noon ET](https://ew.com/avengers-doomsday-cover-story-exclusive-12274201). Interviews were conducted earlier, including around July's Comic-Con, rather than all today.

- EW places Victor in the *First Steps* universe and describes Reed and Victor as former university friends. Joe Russo emphasizes their mutual regard.
- Joe discusses a Latverian witch coven associated with Doom's dark magic; individual identities remain undisclosed.
- Feige describes the two Avengers movies as a transition to a new MCU era.
