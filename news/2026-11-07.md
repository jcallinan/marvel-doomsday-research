**Book-related follow-up, REPORTED (original secondary accounts November 6–7):** [ComicBookMovie's November 6 discussion](https://comicbookmovie.com/avengers/avengers-doomsday/avengers-doomsday-commercial-unveils-latveria-castle-doom-first-look-at-villains-book-of-spells-a230838) and [November 7 account](https://comicbookmovie.com/avengers/avengers-doomsday/avengers-doomsday-promo-art-reveals-full-map-of-latveria-and-confirms-doctor-dooms-earth-828-connection-a230855) attribute new detail to DK's *From Endgame to Doomsday: A Marvel Studios Visual Dictionary*: Doom is represented as a Latverian ruler with a substantial past involving the Fantastic Four, and his magical research involves a **Book of Spells**; the reports interpret him as originating in **Earth-828**. [Penguin Random House's publisher listing](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/827365/from-endgame-to-doomsday-a-marvel-studios-visual-dictionary-avengers-allies-and-foes-by-liz-marsham/) confirms the licensed **128-page book**, authors, and its **November 10 U.S. release**, **not** these particular circulated passages, book-page provenance, or a precise Doom birthplace. The older **November 5 reported five-team synopsis** is already archived in [the prior entry](2026-11-05.md); this is **additional reported character/background material, not a second synopsis announcement**.

**Comic comparisons, not movie revelations:** Sovereign Latveria and its capital draw on the material expanded in *Fantastic Four Annual* #2 (1964) and *Books of Doom* (2005–06). Doom's combination of magic and technology evokes *Doctor Strange & Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment* (1989). An **Earth-828 Fantastic Four history**, even if the reported book wording proves authentic, would **not** on its own establish that RDJ plays Tony Stark, a Tony variant, or a disguised Stark. Likewise the fan map does not prove that the film's final battle occurs in Latveria, that the Citadel appears in-film, or that God Emperor Doom rules Battleworld.
