## 1. Marvel Japan's regional promotional videos and events (October 28–29)

**CONFIRMED official regional publicity; new worldwide theatrical scenes NOT verified.**

- **October 28:** [Marvel Japan announced](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/movie/avengers-doomsday/news/20261028_01) that **Koji Mukai** (Snow Man) joined **Yuki Yamada** as an Avengers ambassador in Japan, with a newly published **promotional interview video** featuring Mukai and an announcement of a pre-release fan event. Mukai discusses his own anticipation of Doom's motives and wonders whether the actor's previous Iron Man association has significance. These remarks are **a fan/ambassador's interpretations**, **not** a Marvel Studios plot disclosure, Tony Stark identity confirmation or new in-film footage. The news article carries an embedded official interview, but its video ID, duration and frame-level contents were not independently established.
- **October 29:** [Marvel Japan published a localized MLB crossover feature/video](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/movie/avengers-doomsday/news/20261029_01) connected to the World Series conclusion. It describes Reed Richards' stadium first pitch, MLB players, Doom's appearance, and Steve Rogers/Thor/Ant-Man/Sam Wilson material already documented from October 23–25 U.S. promotions. **Official Japanese publication date is October 29; the underlying creative originated earlier.** This has **not** been established as globally new or meaningfully altered movie footage. Different narration/localization does not by itself constitute a new cinematic scene.
- **Japan theatrical date:** Both of these [Marvel Japan](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/movie/avengers-doomsday) pages maintain **December 18, 2026** for Japan. This is distinct from **India December 17** ([Marvel India's October 28 announcement](https://x.com/Marvel_India/status/2115315582094692356)) and **U.S. December 18** ([Disney U.S.](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday)).

## 2. Hot Toys releases new Sentinel collectible specifications (announced October 28)

**CONFIRMED licensed merchandise announcement; MOVIE PLOT interpretation NOT confirmed.**

[Hot Toys' official distribution listing at Sideshow](https://www.sideshow.com/collectibles/marvel-sentinel-jumbo-sofvi-hot-toys-915775) documents a **Sentinel Jumbo Sofvi** figure for *Avengers: Doomsday* (manufacturer code **SMS004J**): approximately **60 cm**, purple/gray body, changeable face/chest panels, switchable illuminated eye colors and an approximately **11.5 cm Doctor Doom miniature**. [Toyark's dated October 28 coverage](https://www.toyark.com/2026/10/28/avengers-doomsday-hot-toys-sentinel-jumbo-sofvi-figure-601747) establishes the announcement date and reproduces the manufacturer product description.

The manufacturer markets a base pose as evoking Doom raising Sentinels. That is **promotional product positioning**, *not* independent verification of Doom's command structure, exact on-screen staging, character survival, or a newly released film clip. Prototype photos and diorama accessories should not be treated as theatrical frames.

**Comic context (not film proof):** Sentinels debuted in [*X-Men* #14 (1965)](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/12458/read). Doom's association with these machines is a **marketing inference** until explicitly established in the movie.

## 3. *Captain America* #16: Doom's bargain with Mephisto (comic on sale October 28)

**CONFIRMED comic issue/publication; COMIC PLOT DETAILS: reported in an October 28 issue review; no MCU announcement. Spoilers below.**

[*Captain America* #16](https://comicpreviews.com/series/captain-america-2025), **Chip Zdarsky / Valerio Schiti**, was on sale **October 28, 2026**. [SuperHeroHype's October 28 issue analysis](https://www.superherohype.com/comics/702178-captain-america-makes-doctor-doom-more-dangerous-than-ever-before-avengers-doomsday) describes the conclusion of the **"Hell's Angel"** storyline: Steve Rogers and Doom exploit a magical trap to force a bargain with Mephisto; Doom acquires control of Mephisto's realm and frees those held there. The piece also reports unintended consequences, including the Red Skull's escape. These are **comic continuity developments**, not evidence that Steve and Doom make this bargain in *Avengers: Doomsday*.

**Related but unconfirmed extrapolation:** Other outlets infer that this could affect older Mephisto bargains (including *Spider-Man: One More Day*), but no specific cinematic consequence or Spider-Man continuity revision is established by this research. Cross-check with the primary issue before making comprehensive continuity claims.

See the dedicated [comic issue spotlight](../comics/storylines/captain-america-hells-angel-2026.md) and the existing [Cynthia von Doom theory ledger](../theories/cynthia-von-doom-casting.md): the comic strengthens the **reading-list relevance** of Doom's mystical, Mephisto-facing stories, but does **not** raise confidence that Cynthia, Mephisto or a Hell storyline appears in the MCU film.
