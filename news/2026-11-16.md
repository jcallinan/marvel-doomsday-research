## November 16 — Feige and Joe Russo contrast *Doomsday* with *Secret Wars* (ON-RECORD VIA ATTRIBUTED REPORTING)

[The Playlist, original November 16 at 9:59 a.m.](https://theplaylist.net/kevin-feige-joe-russo-avengers-secret-wars-20261116/) attributes new comments to an *Empire* interview: **Kevin Feige** calls *Avengers: Secret Wars* "a whole other genre of story" relative to *Avengers: Doomsday*; director **Joe Russo** describes an especially unusual world and a deliberately different cinematic experience. This concerns **the 2027 sequel**, not a fresh scene description or plot disclosure from the December 2026 film. The original complete *Empire* article was not independently inspected; treat the remarks as on-record **as reported by The Playlist**, not firsthand access to its full interview.

**What this establishes:** filmmakers intend a substantial shift in approach between the two films. **What it does not establish:** that Battleworld is the setting, that the 1984 Beyonder appears, that the 2015 God Emperor Doom rules a patchwork reality, or that *Doomsday* ends with a specific character's victory. [Dark Horizons' separate November 16 pickup](https://www.darkhorizons.com/avengers-secret-wars-switches-genres/) is additional corroborating **coverage**, not a second independent studio announcement.

**Comics relevance:** In [*Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* (1984–85)](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/features/marvel-secret-wars-explained-avengers-event-mcu/) a Beyonder-created Battleworld gathers combatants; Hickman's [*Secret Wars* (2015)](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/features/marvel-secret-wars-explained-avengers-event-mcu/) depicts a distinct Battleworld following multiversal incursions, with Doom governing it. Both are thematic candidates for a different story-world; **the interview does not choose either**. Updated [Secret Wars evidence ledger](../theories/secret-wars-connections.md) and [Battleworld theory](../theories/battleworld.md).
