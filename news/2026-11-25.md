## November 24, 2 p.m. ET — Feige describes the setting of Secret Wars

**ON RECORD — executive/director interview:** [Nick Romano, Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/avengers-secret-wars-launching-pad-2028-movies-imagination-gone-wild-exclusive-12263231). Feige describes the 2027 movie as occurring on one planet containing different worlds and settings, following multiversal catastrophe and Doom's response. Joe Russo explains that the character and scale require a two-film story. This supports the **Battleworld-style setting of Secret Wars**, not an authenticated Doomsday scene. EW identifies the concept as Battleworld; Feige's quoted description establishes the setting rather than the exact on-screen name, geography, creator, ruler, or power source. No confirmation of the God Emperor title, Loki as a power source, the Beyonder, Molecule Man, or a particular ending. [Revised theory ledger](../theories/battleworld.md).
