**What changed:** The *mid-film Doom introduction* and early outreach details specify the **abandoned development path**, beyond the earlier general knowledge that the studio considered Doom in 2023. Marvel has not confirmed Kang appears in *Doomsday*. Feige's current identification of Downey's character as **Victor von Doom, not a Tony Stark variant** was already archived from **November 12**; it is not new news today.
