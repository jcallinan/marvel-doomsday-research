## Doctor Doom and comic connections (THEORY, no confidence upgrade)

- The [Jeep release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jeep-brand-takes-adventure-to-marvel-studios-avengers-doomsday-universe-in-epic-finale-of-sweeping-yearlong-global-collaboration-302931380.html) makes **Latveria** a confirmed *advertising* destination. The fictional country is deeply established in Marvel comics: see [Marvel's `Books of Doom` collected edition](https://www.marvel.com/comics/collection/124641/doctor_doom_books_of_doom_2025), charting Victor's eventual throne, and [`One World Under Doom` #1](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/113858/read) for Doom's rule over Latveria. It is **plausible** Latveria will be important to the film; an official ad location alone **does not confirm an in-film establishing shot** or specific Doomstadt architecture.
- Multiple worlds/portal effects in partner marketing invite comparison to Hickman's `New Avengers` incursions and `Secret Wars` (2015); **no official mechanism, portal operator, Battleworld, or God Emperor Doom** is established.
- The world-tour video and Latverian aesthetic do not substantiate a Tony Stark / Victor identity-swap theory.
