## New official Japan localization, casting and promotional videos — November 3

**CONFIRMED — new Japanese-language voice casting, not new live-action casting.** [Marvel Japan's dated November 3 press release](https://marvel.disney.co.jp/movie/avengers-doomsday/news/20261103_01) announces **Toshiyuki Morikawa (森川智之)** as the **Japanese dubbing voice** for Robert Downey Jr.'s **Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom**. Marvel had previously revealed 29 other Japanese-language performers; Doom's Japanese performer was withheld until this announcement. Downey remains the on-screen actor. The studio announcement documents Morikawa's interview and Downey's acknowledgement of the late **Keiji Fujiwara**, who previously voiced Downey's Tony Stark in Japanese MCU releases. This is an official localization announcement, **not** a revelation that MCU Tony Stark and Victor von Doom are the same person.
