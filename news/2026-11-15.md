Marvel Entertainment's official YouTube channel published [**Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday | Every Moment on Disney+**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kl7nG1nD0mk) on **November 13, 2026**. [GridVids' Marvel-channel archive](https://gridvids.com/marvel-entertainment/countdown-to-avengers-doomsday-every-moment-on-disney) and [Trari's November 14 channel tracking](https://trari.com/en/story/marvel-36374/) independently identify the official channel, title and original date. The video promotes the Disney+ Countdown collection and the film's December 18 U.S. premiere. It is **not a newly announced full theatrical trailer**.

This is **a different upload** from the official [November 11 Disney+ retrospective (3pnpz8SqRI8)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3pnpz8SqRI8) and the [Disney UK November 12 television spot (7n3etujuU3c)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7n3etujuU3c). Separate video IDs establish separate published edits, **not** the amount of novel film footage.

**REPORTED shot/audio observations, not direct independent playback:** [9to5Marvel, originally November 14](https://www.9to5marvel.com/doctor-doom-new-line-avengers-doomsday-spot/) and [Pirates & Princesses' November 13 coverage](https://news.piratesandprincesses.net/p/avengers-doomsday-teaser-gives-doctor) describe a roughly one-minute archival MCU/X-Men montage ending on a close-up of Doctor Doom's eyes and the line **“Now you must answer to me. For I am Doom.”** The ending is broadly reported as the final approximately 10–11 seconds; **do not interpret that range as an independently measured timecode**. The provenance of the dialogue audio as a final theatrical-cut line has **not** been established. The described Jean Grey image has been linked by viewers to previously released **X2** footage, not to a newly filmed Phoenix scene ([November 13 fan comparison](https://www.reddit.com/r/NewRockstars/comments/1xvj0mb/who_is_this/)). The exact X2 source-frame match was **not independently frame-verified** here; treat the strong archival identification as REPORTED, not a new casting announcement.

**Comics interpretation:** Doom's absolutist wording is consistent with his Latverian-monarch persona and with his God Emperor characterization in *Secret Wars* (2015). It **does not establish** that the movie depicts Battleworld, has Doom seize Wanda's power, or features Phoenix. See [updated video catalog](../mcu/trailers-and-promos.md).

## Original November 15 — Owen Wilson/Mobius return claim (ATTRIBUTED INTERVIEW; not newly authenticated casting)

[Comic Basics, published November 15](https://www.comicbasics.com/another-marvel-tv-character-confirms-his-return-in-avengers-doomsday/), says **Owen Wilson** hinted to **ET Now** that Mobius returns in *Doomsday*. This account is stronger than a fan casting wish, but **the alleged ET Now interview recording/transcript was not independently located**, nor did the [official Disney cast page](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) newly list Wilson in this check. Classify as **REPORTED / interview attributed**, **not official casting confirmation or proof of specific new movie footage**.

**Previously known, do not count twice:** [Disney's September 29 official *Endgame: Encore* feature](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/russo-brothers-avengers-endgame-doomsday/) explicitly identifies Wilson's **Mobius** in the already screened TVA footage, along with Casey and O.B. Wilson had also discussed that September footage in an [October 1 ComingSoon interview](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2200814-avengers-endgame-encore-owen-wilson-mcu-scene-doomsday-directors). The November 15 secondary claim does not by itself establish a distinct *Doomsday* movie scene.

**Comics boundary:** Mobius and the TVA are connected to comic time-management institutions; their MCU role in cross-universe crises is plausible, but a new comic adaptation, TVA alliance with Doom, or final film outcome is **not confirmed**.

## Historical-source accuracy correction discovered during this November 15 check

[The Walt Disney Company's **September 29, 2026** feature](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/russo-brothers-avengers-endgame-doomsday/) had **already used the specific term “incursions”**, describing the TVA's multiverse crisis. The more recent **November 13** Feige interview likewise uses the term in the context of Avengers facing incursions, but it is **not the first official use of the word**. Prior November 14 archive wording implying Feige introduced the term for the first time should be read with this correction. This does not verify the comics' precise physics or Doom's role in causing incursions.
