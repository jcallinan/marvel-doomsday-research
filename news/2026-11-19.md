## November 18 — official 11-poster character set (CONFIRMED STUDIO MARKETING, NOT FOOTAGE)

Marvel Studios posted the set on **November 18** with the caption “In ONE MONTH, Doomsday arrives”: [original studio post](https://x.com/MarvelStudios/status/2123114012657295484); [contemporaneous Nov. 18 report displaying the set](https://www.9to5marvel.com/avengers-doomsday-character-posters-pairings/); [November 19 independent rundown](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/english/news/2026/Nov/19/avengers-doomsday-marvel-pairs-gambit-with-yelena-magneto-with-black-panther-in-new-posters).

**Reported set of eleven:** ten dual-character images featuring **Thor / Steve Rogers; Sam Wilson / Cyclops; Magneto / Shuri; Professor X / Reed Richards; Loki / Mystique; Gambit / Yelena; Sue Storm / Namor; Bucky Barnes / Shang-Chi; Ant-Man / Red Guardian; M'Baku / Nightcrawler**, plus **one solo Doctor Doom image**. The assemblage is new **marketing art**, not an authenticated film scene, and its layout neither confirms the indicated characters fight together nor establishes anyone's death or allegiance. Reporters note a new official promotional look at Alan Cumming's Nightcrawler; his **casting had been announced much earlier**. Some interpret background robot designs as Doombots or Sentinels; this archive has **not individually identified artwork elements from first-party high-resolution assets**, so no new film robot variant is recorded as confirmed.

**Comic interpretation:** Doom as the isolated central antagonist and an unprecedented crossover of teams broadly evoke *Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* (1984–85) and Hickman's *Secret Wars* (2015). **Battleworld, God Emperor Doom, a Beyonder, the Maker and every poster-implied on-screen alliance are still unconfirmed**. [Existing theory](../theories/battleworld.md).
