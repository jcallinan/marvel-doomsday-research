## November 10–11 — *VisionQuest* finale directly connects Doctor Doom and Scarlet Witch (ON SCREEN, corroborated; major new MCU story development)

[Disney+'s official *VisionQuest* show page](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-8744ec55-8d14-4631-99b4-c889a2d6013f) is the authorized place to watch the episode. Disney's [episode rollout guide](https://www.disneyplus.com/explore/articles/marvel-visionquest) lists Episode 8 for **November 11**. [Entertainment Weekly, **Nov. 10 at 11:01 p.m. ET**](https://ew.com/visionquest-avengers-doomsday-post-credits-scene-elizabeth-olsen-scarlet-witch-return-12248438), [People, **Nov. 11**](https://people.com/visionquest-finale-tease-this-unexpected-character-return-surprise-end-credit-scene-12248528), and [TheWrap, **Nov. 11**](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/tv-shows/wanda-alive-visionquestion-post-credits-scene-reactions/) describe the **previously withheld post-credits scene**:

- The sequence revisits Wanda Maximoff's apparent death beneath Mount Wundagore in *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*.
- **Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom** moves the rubble and addresses **Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch**; Doom states his wish to obtain her power. Wanda opens her eyes.
- The *VisionQuest* finale also reunites **Vision, Billy and Tommy**, making Wanda's return a particularly direct setup for a potential Maximoff-family conflict. The family reunion itself occurs within *VisionQuest*, **not a confirmed scene from *Avengers: Doomsday***.
- The secret scene was **not included in advance press screeners**, according to EW. Its reveal is a substantive **new MCU continuity development**, not recycled promotional art or a fabricated leak.

**Scope and casting caveat:** The episode establishes **Olsen's appearance as Wanda in *VisionQuest*** and **Downey's appearance as Doom there**, with an obvious narrative bridge to *Doomsday*. It **does not independently establish Olsen in the official *Doomsday* film cast**, nor prove that Doom has already stolen her magic, that Wanda willingly allies with him, that she is a named “Latverian Witch,” or that Battleworld exists. [Disney's *Doomsday* cast list](https://movies.disney.com/avengers-doomsday) did not list Olsen at this check. The post-credit scene is **television episode footage, not a newly uploaded standalone *Doomsday* theatrical trailer**. ### Film theory connections (THEORY, not adaptation confirmation)

The clearest comic precedent is Allan Heinberg and Jim Cheung's ***Avengers: The Children's Crusade* (2010–2012)**. [Marvel's own Young Avengers history](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/young-avengers) recounts Billy and Tommy's search for Wanda, her discovery in **Latveria** with Doom, and Doom's later attempt to seize her reality-altering power. [Marvel's *Children's Crusade* #3 entry, originally **November 10, 2010**](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/31138/avengers-the-childrens-crusade-2010-3), frames the search for Wanda alongside a villain's interest in possessing her; [#5, **April 6, 2011**](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/31142/read), explicitly names the Latveria/Doom conflict. The similarity is now **evidence-backed as a thematic parallel**, but there is no verified one-to-one film adaptation, canon engagement/romance, amnesia arc or ability transfer.

This new scene makes **Doom targeting Wanda's power** a directly grounded MCU setup; it does **not** prove the use of those powers to cause incursions, fabricate Battleworld, imitate God Emperor Doom, or conceal a Tony Stark identity. See [the dedicated evidence ledger](../theories/doom-scarlet-witch.md) and [the comics background](../comics/storylines/avengers-the-childrens-crusade-2010.md).
