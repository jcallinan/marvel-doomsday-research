## November 12 — Feige explicitly distinguishes Victor von Doom from Tony Stark (ON RECORD; archived November 13)

In [Empire's November 12 exclusive interview](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/avengers-doomsday-doctor-doom-worth-being-scared-of-exclusive/), Marvel Studios president **Kevin Feige** rejected the premise that casting Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom is a shortcut to portraying a **Tony Stark variant**. He identifies the character as **Victor von Doom** and compares actor reuse to Chris Evans portraying both Steve Rogers and Johnny Storm. Contemporaneous corroboration: [SlashFilm, Nov. 12](https://www.slashfilm.com/2315324/robert-downey-jr-doctor-doom-not-tony-stark-variant-marvel/) and [People, Nov. 12](https://people.com/robert-downey-jr-opens-up-about-his-first-time-as-avengers-villain-12251314).

**Interpretation boundary:** This substantially **rebuts the literal Tony-is-Doom film-identity hypothesis** and supports a distinct Victor. It does **not** rule out visual lookalike jokes, emotional parallels, misidentification by Avengers, or hidden multiverse subplots not addressed in the interview. It also does not mean Marvel has ruled out every instance of Tony Stark appearing elsewhere.

## November 12 — Russo brothers describe bereavement and sorcery (ON RECORD; archived November 13)

In the same [Empire exclusive](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/avengers-doomsday-doctor-doom-worth-being-scared-of-exclusive/), **Joe Russo** describes Doom as a grieving **father who has lost a child** and portrays him as a dangerous magical/Gothic antagonist. **Anthony Russo** describes Doom's motives as more ego-driven than Thanos's. These statements are reported by [SlashFilm, original Nov. 12](https://www.slashfilm.com/2315324/robert-downey-jr-doctor-doom-not-tony-stark-variant-marvel/) and [People's original Nov. 12 report of the Downey interview](https://people.com/robert-downey-jr-opens-up-about-his-first-time-as-avengers-villain-12251314).

**Confirmed as on-record characterization, not visually demonstrated facts:** The filmmakers' statements concern the movie's characterization; the **child's identity, fate, timeline and possible relation to Franklin or Valeria Richards have not been authenticated**. There is no evidence of an actual Mephisto deal or that the film adapts a specific comic panel. `Doctor Strange & Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment` (1989) shows Doom pursuing a family member's soul through Hell. [Marvel's Mephisto reading guide](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/2503/mephistos-most-impactful-deals) explicitly describes the mother/Mephisto story; **a mother's captivity is not the same as the film's alleged lost child**. [Expanded comics dossier](../comics/storylines/doom-sorcery-and-grief-1989.md).

Downey also discusses embracing the villain role rather than returning as Iron Man; see [People's Nov. 12 attributed interview recap](https://people.com/robert-downey-jr-opens-up-about-his-first-time-as-avengers-villain-12251314).
