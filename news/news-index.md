## Daily update rules

Check primary sources, avoid duplicates, classify claims as **confirmed / reported / rumor / theory**, and cite original publication dates. Differentiate an older event newly documented in the repository from a genuinely new development. If nothing substantive occurred, do not fabricate a daily entry or claim an unverified trailer.
