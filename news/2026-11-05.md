**Source chain and limit:** [Penguin Random House/DK's official book listing](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/827365/from-endgame-to-doomsday-a-marvel-studios-visual-dictionary-avengers-allies-and-foes-by-liz-marsham/) **confirms the book exists**, its title, authors Liz Marsham, Amy Ratcliffe and Melanie Scott, and **U.S. publication date November 10, 2026** (128 pages). GamesRadar+ explicitly bases the passage on a fan-account post, not a publisher-hosted scan/excerpt that this archive independently inspected. Accordingly the **expanded wording and team list remain REPORTED, not newly CONFIRMED Marvel/Disney film canon**. The reported book text adds named organizations and tension between them. It does **not** name an incursion, Battleworld, God Emperor Doom, the villain's final strategy or which universe survives.
