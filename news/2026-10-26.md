**Classification: CONFIRMED — official co-promotion, with an important footage caveat.**

## 2. Jonathan Hickman on the film's development (October 21 interview)

**Classification: REPORTED — on-record interview with the comic writer, not an official Marvel plot announcement.**

Speaking to Bleeding Cool, Jonathan Hickman said he has read *Doomsday* scripts during development, discussed significant changes when the Russo brothers, screenwriter Stephen McFeely and Robert Downey Jr. returned to the project, and described seeing work-in-progress visual-effects material. He explicitly said he had **not** seen a final cut. He described general studio consulting, but the interview **does not explicitly establish a credited consulting role on both films**, despite stronger interpretations elsewhere.

- **Original interview:** [Bleeding Cool / Gavin Sheehan (2026-10-21)](https://bleedingcool.com/comics/lucca-jonathan-hickman-works-on-more-films-than-anyone-will-know/).
- **Comic connection — COMIC CANON:** Hickman's *Fantastic Four*, *Avengers*, *New Avengers* and *Secret Wars* (2015) are valuable comparison texts for inter-universe conflict and Doom's ambitions.
- **Film inference — THEORY:** Access to scripts does not prove that the films reproduce the comics' incursions, Battleworld, or God Emperor Doom.
