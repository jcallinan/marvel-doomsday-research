# Doctor Doom: curated reading order

This is a starting guide, **not** a complete appearance database. Publication order differs from in-universe chronology.

| Order | Work | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Fantastic Four* #5 (1962) | First Doom appearance |
| 2 | *Fantastic Four Annual* #2 (1964) | Early expanded origin and Latveria |
| 3 | *Fantastic Four* #57–60 (1966–67) | Silver Surfer power storyline |
| 4 | *Super-Villain Team-Up* (1975 series), selected issues | Doom as a ruler and political player |
| 5 | *Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* #1–12 (1984–85) | Cosmic power and the Beyonder |
| 6 | *Doctor Strange & Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment* (1989) | Mysticism and Doom's mother |
| 7 | *Books of Doom* #1–6 (2005–06) | Modern origin retelling |
| 8 | Hickman's *Fantastic Four* and *Avengers/New Avengers* runs (selected) | Multiversal context |
| 9 | *Secret Wars* #1–9 (2015–16) | Battleworld and God Emperor Doom |
| 10 | *Infamous Iron Man* #1–12 (2016–17) | Doom's attempted reinvention |
| 11 | *One World Under Doom* (2025) | Modern global-scale Doom narrative |

See [storyline guides](storylines/) and [bibliography](../sources/bibliography.md). The 1984 and 2015 *Secret Wars* events are different works.
