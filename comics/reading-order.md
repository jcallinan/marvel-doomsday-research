# Doctor Doom: curated reading order

**November 25, 2026 research addition (historical comic, not new publication):** Read *Thors* (2015) #1–4 after Hickman's *Secret Wars*. [Marvel's June 29, 2020 Jason Aaron retrospective](https://www.marvel.com/articles/comics/the-full-reading-order-of-jason-aaron-s-thor) documents the alternate Thors' law-enforcement role in the composite realm and the murder investigation; [publisher issue #2](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/52825/thors_2015_2) was published July 29, 2015 (Aaron/Sprouse). [Feige's Nov. 24 interview](https://ew.com/avengers-secret-wars-launching-pad-2028-movies-imagination-gone-wild-exclusive-12263231) now describes a Battleworld-style setting in the **2027 sequel**, superseding earlier uncertainty on that narrow premise. The Nov. 24 Korean teaser's reported Thor/Doom confrontation is not evidence for Thor Corps or a specific adaptation. [Dated evidence](../news/2026-11-25.md).

**November 24, 2026 — priority reading update (historical comics):** [EW's new filmmaker interviews](https://ew.com/avengers-doomsday-cover-story-exclusive-12274201) make the Hickman prelude a higher-priority research path; see [source summary](../news/2026-11-24.md). Read *Avengers* (2012) #35–44 and *New Avengers* (2013) #24–33, then [*Secret Wars* (2015) #1–9](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/508/secret-wars). Publisher issue checks: [*Avengers* #37 — October 22, 2014](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/48391/avengers_2012_37), [#40 — January 14, 2015](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/48394/avengers_2012_40). For Victor and Reed, revisit *Fantastic Four Annual* #2 and *Books of Doom* with [Marvel's Fantastic Four background](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/fantastic-four/in-comics) and [Future Foundation history](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/future-foundation). For mystical themes retain the [1989 Doom/Strange dossier](storylines/doom-sorcery-and-grief-1989.md) and [Children's Crusade dossier](storylines/avengers-the-childrens-crusade-2010.md). This is a reading prioritization, not new issue releases or a guaranteed adaptation of any individual event.

**November 23, 2026 trailer research insertion (not new comic publications):** After the [official *Avengers: Doomsday* final trailer debuted Nov. 23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wb8Tq_HD4E8), read *Uncanny X-Men* **#141–142 (1981)** for Sentinel-controlled alternate-future mythology ([Marvel's official #142 facsimile page](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/113166/uncanny_x-men_facsimile_edition_2023_142)), then **#145 (1981)** for a [publisher-documented X-Men/Doctor Doom encounter](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/13686). [Marvel's X-Men background](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/x-men/in-comics) credits Bolivar Trask with Sentinels; **a film trailer placing them near Doom does not establish Victor invented them**. For the **Kate Bishop** cast announcement (**official Nov. 22**, not Nov. 23), add [*Young Avengers* (2005) #1–12](https://www.marvel.com/comics/guides/1852/young-avengers) and [Marvel's Kate Bishop guide](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/2209/kate-bishops-best-hits); **Young Avengers formation in the movie is not confirmed**. Preserve [*Secret Wars* (2015)](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/508/secret-wars) as an optional **comic comparison**, not a verified Battleworld adaptation. [Complete issue-focused dossier](storylines/doomsday-2026-final-trailer-comics-evidence.md); [source/event date audit](../news/2026-11-23.md).
