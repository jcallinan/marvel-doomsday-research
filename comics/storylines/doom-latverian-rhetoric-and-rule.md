# Doctor Doom: Latverian rule, public rhetoric and political theater

**Research entry created:** November 20, 2026. **Comics publication dates:** 2025 / earlier historical issues. **Prompt for research:** November 20 coverage of RDJ's *Empire*-reported interview and November 17–18 licensed Dunkin activations. These dates do **not** indicate that the comic was new this week.

## First-party comic anchors

1. **[One World Under Doom (2025) #1, Marvel Comics](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/123518/one_world_under_doom_2025_1_variant/variant)** — issue published **February 12, 2025**, written by Ryan North with R. B. Silva art. Marvel's issue synopsis describes Victor, at that point Sorcerer Supreme, making a global broadcast declaring a new United Latveria and presenting himself as emperor. **Publisher-confirmed comic premise**, not a synopsis of the film.
2. **[Doctor Doom (2019) #8, Marvel Comics](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/78527/doctor_doom_8)** — issue published **October 28, 2020**, by Christopher Cantwell and Salvador Larroca. Marvel's synopsis depicts Doom regaining Latveria's throne and invading Symkaria while treating himself as the only person capable of averting a larger catastrophe. **Comic-canon political self-justification**, not a newly filmed MCU plot.
3. **[Marvel's Fantastic Four comics overview](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/fantastic-four/in-comics)** — historical background on Doom's rivalry with Reed Richards and the team's conflicts. Useful to frame his public persona against personal grievance; not evidence of a specific film scene.
