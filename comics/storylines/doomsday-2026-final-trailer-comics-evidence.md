## Track A — Sentinels: Trask origin versus possible Doom deployment

**Publisher fact:** Marvel's [X-Men character/team history](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/x-men/in-comics) credits **Bolivar Trask** with the first Sentinels and describes their anti-mutant function. These are historically **not a Doctor Doom invention**. **“Days of Future Past”:** [Marvel's reprint of *Uncanny X-Men* #142](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/113166/uncanny_x-men_facsimile_edition_2023_142) recalls the surviving X-Men battling Sentinels in a dystopian alternate future (original comic story **1981**, facsimile dated **November 29, 2023**). [Marvel's X-Men alternate-timelines guide](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/2519/the-x-mens-major-alternate-timelines) identifies *X-Men* #141 as the opening of that story.

**Film observation, qualified:** November 23 reviews place **Doom and Sentinels** amid the same X-Men confrontation. **Unconfirmed interpretation:** Doom could command, capture, upgrade, manipulate, or simply fight near machines built by someone else. Even if a journalist calls them “Doom's Sentinels,” that cannot establish their **inventor, ownership, chain of command or relationship to the MCU's Trask** without identifiable dialogue or an explicit official synopsis. **Reading priority:** *Uncanny X-Men* #141–142 before assuming the film's sentinels are a new Doom creation.

## Track B — Doom's established encounters with the X-Men

Marvel's original [*Uncanny X-Men* #145](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/13686), **May 1981**, describes the team rescuing Arcade from Doom. This shows **Victor has a historical X-Men comics relationship** outside *Secret Wars*. It supports a thematic comparator to Doom battling mutants on screen, but **not a claim the film borrows that plot**. Also compare [*Secret Wars* (2015)](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/508/secret-wars) for the much larger cross-franchise narrative framework.

## Track C — Steve/Peggy family versus alternate-reality stakes

Reports say Steve and Peggy have a teenage son and are approached by the Fantastic Four. This makes **family secrecy and the consequences of choices** legitimate observable promotional themes. It does **not** establish the boy's comics identity, an alternate-universe code, whether Doom is motivated by this family, which timeline survives, or any death. Treat all such claims as **theories pending a film scene or authoritative script/creator explanation**.

## Track D — Kate Bishop / Young Avengers

[Marvel's *Young Avengers* (2005) reading guide](https://www.marvel.com/comics/guides/1852/young-avengers) identifies **Kate Bishop (Hawkeye)** as a member of the foundational series. [Marvel's Young Avengers history](https://www.marvel.com/teams-and-groups/young-avengers) also lists **Cassie Lang**, showing their comic relationship. **Film inference limit:** Kate's newly official return is **casting evidence**, not an announcement that the MCU's **Young Avengers team** assembles or that a separate project is greenlit. Her presence could equally be part of a general crossover ensemble.

## Track E — Hickman incursions and God Emperor Doom

[Marvel's *Secret Wars* (2015) official reading guide](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/508/secret-wars) describes a final incursion of the Marvel and Ultimate universes and the ensuing patchwork **Battleworld**. Combined with already established Disney/Feige references to incursions ([original Disney September 29 account](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/russo-brothers-avengers-endgame-doomsday/)), the new trailer's team crossover is **consistent with** the general multiverse-conflict premise. It is **not evidence that Doom has become God Emperor, created Battleworld, taken Beyonder powers, or re-created precisely the 2015 event**.
