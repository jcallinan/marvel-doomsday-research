## Why this matters to a Doctor Doom reading order

Doom, Namor and Black Panther are **sovereigns** rather than merely costumed combatants. Their conflicts can be about national control, power, grudges and reluctant bargaining, unlike the all-heroes-vs-one-villain structure of many cinematic team-ups. This current publication is relevant background for Doom as a political ruler, his relationship with Namor, and supernatural motifs, alongside earlier material including *Doctor Strange & Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment* (1989) and *Secret Wars* (2015). It does **not** change those older books' publication dates.
