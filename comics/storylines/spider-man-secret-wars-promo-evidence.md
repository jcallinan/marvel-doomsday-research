# Spider-Man, *Secret Wars* (1984), and the limits of crossover-marketing evidence

**Research added November 22, 2026.** These are **historical published comic works**, not comics released this week. Related: [Nov. 22 source audit](../../news/2026-11-22.md), [film theories](../../theories/secret-wars-connections.md).

## Publisher-confirmed comic baseline

- ***Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* #8 (1984), by Jim Shooter and Mike Zeck:** Marvel's [official 2024 facsimile issue description](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/112789/marvel_super_heroes_secret_wars_8_facsimile_edition_2024_8) places Spider-Man in a new black costume on the original *Secret Wars* Battleworld, fighting alongside an assembled crossover of heroes facing Galactus. **The facsimile was issued August 14, 2024; the underlying comic is from 1984**, not a November 2026 comic.
- ***Spider-Man: The Complete Alien Costume Saga* collection (2014):** Marvel's [publisher listing](https://www.marvel.com/comics/collection/48111/spider-man_the_complete_alien_costume_saga_book_trade_paperback) traces Spider-Man's return from the original *Secret Wars* with the alien black suit and collects *Amazing Spider-Man* #252–258, *Marvel Team-Up* #141–145 plus Annual #7, and *Peter Parker, The Spectacular Spider-Man* #90–95. **July 30, 2014** is the collected-edition publication date, not the original story date.
- **Different 2015 event:** Jonathan Hickman's *Secret Wars* is an **incursions/patchwork-reality** narrative with God Emperor Doom, distinct from the Beyonder's original 1984 battlefield. [Marvel's 2015 reading guide](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/508/secret-wars) is a **comic synopsis**, not MCU confirmation.

## Why the November 19, 2026 sports promo does not settle Spider-Man casting

[B.LEAGUE's original October 16 licensed-campaign announcement](https://www.bleague.jp/en/news_detail/id%3D666310) and [Marvel Studios Japan's originally November 19 co-promotional video](https://x.com/MarvelStudios_J/status/2123270445306962163) establish a **licensed advertising campaign**. [The Direct's November 22 description](https://thedirect.com/article/avengers-doomsday-tom-holland-spider-man-marvel-studios-tobey-maguire-mcu) identifies a **reused 2019 *Avengers: Endgame* Spider-Man portal shot** mixed with older *Doomsday* imagery and basketball clips. Original video playback and shot-by-shot timing are **not independently verified** here.

**Logical test:** Inclusion in a regional advertisement of **archive footage featuring Tom Holland** proves only that marketers selected an older recognizable shot. It proves **neither Holland nor Tobey Maguire** acts in *Avengers: Doomsday*. Likewise, the comic history does **not** imply the film will give Peter the black symbiote suit, show the 1984 Battleworld, stage a Spider-Man/Wolverine battle, or kill a Spider-Man variant.

| Claim | Current evidence level | What would substantiate it |
|---|---|---|
| Spider-Man wore a black costume during 1984 comics *Secret Wars* | **Publisher-confirmed historical comics** | Already confirmed by the above Marvel source |
| Marvel and B.LEAGUE ran a *Doomsday* sports crossover | **First-party promotion** | B.LEAGUE original release / Marvel Japan post |
| Holland is in the *Doomsday* feature-film cast | **Unconfirmed** | New authenticated film frame, studio casting announcement or credited participation |
| Maguire is in *Doomsday* | **Rumor; not established by the ad** | Same original-film or studio evidence |
| 1984 or 2015 *Secret Wars* Spider-Man material is adapted in the film | **Speculation** | On-screen film material or explicit creative statement |

**Watch points:** If an upcoming official trailer includes an actually new Spider-Man scene, document **original channel, original publication date, a playable URL, precise verified shot timestamp and which actor can genuinely be identified** before changing film-cast or plot entries. Do not mistake prior-feature archive footage for new theatrical-cut footage.
