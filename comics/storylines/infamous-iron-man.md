# Infamous Iron Man (2016–2017)

**Marvel Comics continuity — not a confirmed adaptation in *Avengers: Doomsday*.** Written by Brian Michael Bendis with art by Alex Maleev. This series depicts **Victor von Doom taking on Iron Man's heroic identity**; it does **not** assert that Victor is Tony Stark.

## Primary issue guide

- **`Infamous Iron Man` #1 — October 19, 2016:** Marvel's [official first-issue variant listing](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/61980/infamous_iron_man_2016_1_ribic_variant) names Victor von Doom as the new Iron Man. This is the clearest starting point for the comic/actor comparison.
- **`Infamous Iron Man` #4 — January 25, 2017:** [Marvel issue summary](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/61550) shows a confrontation with Ben Grimm and frames Victor's attempt to convince others he has changed.
- **`Infamous Iron Man` #7 — April 26, 2017:** [Marvel issue summary](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/61553/infamous_iron_man_2016_7) introduces conflict with the **Maker**, a Reed Richards from a dead reality. Do **not** treat the presence of alternate-reality Reed in comics as a confirmed *Doomsday* cameo.
- **`Infamous Iron Man` #11 — August 30, 2017:** [Marvel issue summary](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/61557/infamous_iron_man_2016_11) involves Victor, his mother and the Maker. This is **comic-canon** context, not new evidence for an MCU Cynthia von Doom or Mephisto story.

## October 31, 2026 promotional parallels (archive review November 1)

- The [Seoul/Disney Korea city drone video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6KNlqWk_Kbw) uses Iron Man and Doctor Doom imagery in close succession ([official chapter metadata](https://allsnsrank.com/youtube/video/6KNlqWk_Kbw): Iron Man at 9:27/9:50; Doom at 10:35). [November 1 reporting](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/robert-downey-jr-iron-man-doctor-doom-seoul-drone-show-1829309) describes the visual transition. This is **promotional symbolism** only.
- Downey and Pedro Pascal's [October 31 Halloween social clip](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfL1wYpByqM/) exchanges Reed/Doom costumes; [November 1 report](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/hollywood/robert-downey-jr-pedro-pascal-cross-dress-as-mister-fantastic-doctor-doom-on-halloween-10945665/). It provides no evidence of identity swaps in the movie.

**Interpretation, not plot confirmation:** the comic lets Victor inherit an Iron Man mantle while remaining Victor, and juxtaposes him with an alternate Reed. Downey's double casting gives the concept thematic appeal, but Disney explicitly identifies the actor's new part as **Victor von Doom**, and promotional art is not a script disclosure. No change to the [Doom/Tony theory assessment](../../theories/doom-and-tony-stark.md).

**Reading order:** Start with #1, continue the 2016–17 limited run, then compare #7 for alternate-Reed themes. Track issue dates separately from this **November 1, 2026 archival expansion**.
