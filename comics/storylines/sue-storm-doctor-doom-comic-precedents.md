# Sue Storm / Doctor Doom: comic-book conflict precedents

**Dossier created (archive check):** November 18, 2026. **Film-interview original news date:** November 17, 2026. **Research status:** Marvel-publisher-identified comics are real; the specific *Avengers: Doomsday* comic scene is **not identified**.

## Why this is worth revisiting now

[Vanessa Kirby's *Empire*-attributed interview, reported by Nerdist November 17](https://nerdist.com/article/vanessa-kirby-used-comic-reference-for-sue-vs-doom-scene-doomsday/), says that imagery of **Sue in conflict with Doctor Doom** which she sent to the Russo brothers informed a key scene close to a comic reference. [ComingSoon's original November 17 account](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2225039-avengers-doomsday-sue-storm-fantastic-four-comics-connection) corroborates this framing. **ON-RECORD VIA SECONDARY ATTRIBUTION**, not independently inspected original magazine pages. Kirby does not publicly identify the issue number in these reports. *Close to* a comic scene does not imply the same dialogue, consequences, powers or characters.

## What would confirm the source

A direct statement from Kirby, Anthony or Joe Russo naming an issue; an official production still accompanied by an issue citation; or an authenticated frame-by-frame comparison after public release. An uncaptioned leaked still, a reviewer guess or Doom/Sue history alone does not suffice.

## Broader Doctor Doom story implications

This evidence adds **specific actor-confirmed interest in Sue's conflict with Victor** to the previously archived [November 13 Pedro Pascal/Reed-Doom characterization](../../theories/reed-doom-rivalry.md). It is consistent with Doom as a personal antagonist to the Fantastic Four, not just a cosmic conqueror. It does **not** establish Sue's death, mind control, a love triangle, an alliance with Doom, Doom targeting Franklin or Valeria, Battleworld, or God Emperor Doom. Joe Russo's previously reported grief/magic notes have their own [comics comparison](doom-sorcery-and-grief-1989.md).
