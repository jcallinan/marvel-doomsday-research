## Reported issue developments (SPOILERS; third-party issue reading)

In the conclusion of "Hell's Angel," Steve Rogers and Doctor Doom confront **Mephisto** inside his realm. The analysis describes Doom having linked Rogers' soul to an anti-demonic weapon, using Mephisto's desire to claim Captain America's soul to put the demon at a disadvantage. Doom leverages the encounter to obtain control of the realm and releases the imprisoned souls. The story is presented as a victory with consequences: the **Red Skull** is among those freed.

**Evidence boundary:** The plot details above come from a published review of Marvel's comic, not from an official *Doomsday* trailer or filmmaker. Any claims about the consequences for *One More Day*, the broader Marvel timeline or future film continuity require additional primary evidence.

## Why this matters for a Doctor Doom reading library

1. **Science, sorcery and bargaining:** Doom attempts to outmaneuver an immensely powerful supernatural opponent; a modern counterpoint to his longstanding ambitions to free his mother from Mephisto.
2. **Steve Rogers as foil and collaborator:** The comic puts Doom and Steve into uneasy cooperation, a theme worth comparing to their **independently established** MCU roles without assuming the same plot.
3. **Moral ambiguity:** Freeing prisoners can be a humanitarian act and create new threats at once; this is a specific 2026 example of Doom imposing an order on reality by force.
4. **Other relevant reading:** *Doctor Strange & Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment* (1989), *Books of Doom* #1–6 (2005–06), and *One World Under Doom* (2025). These are **comic parallels**, not adaptation confirmations.
