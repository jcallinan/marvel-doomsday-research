## What the series is

**CONFIRMED published Marvel material / REPORTED upcoming solicitation:** `Challenges of Doom` is a sequence of five connected Doctor Doom-focused one-shots written by **Al Ewing**, each pairing Victor with a different hero and exploring a different facet of his life and abilities. The series was **announced April 16, 2026**, not November 2. In an [April 16 announcement reported by Comics Beat](https://www.comicsbeat.com/challenges-of-doom-announced/), Marvel executive editor Tom Brevoort described the project as a way of examining Doom's power, personality and intellect. In [Popverse's August 19 creator interview/announcement](https://www.thepopverse.com/comics-challenges-of-doom-dr-strange-announce), Ewing explained that the one-shots are snapshots from different points in Doom's life, intended together to form a broader narrative.
