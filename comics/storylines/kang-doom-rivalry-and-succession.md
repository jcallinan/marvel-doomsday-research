# Kang and Doctor Doom — a comics evidence dossier

**Research/archiving date:** November 17, 2026. **Original comics:** 1963, 1964, 1984–85 and 2021. **Reason for this study:** Kevin Feige's *Empire*-attributed remarks, reported November 16–17, that a proposed *Avengers: The Kang Dynasty* version would have introduced Doom midway. **No new comic publication is being claimed for November 17.**

## Publisher-verifiable starting points

| Publication | Publisher-level source | Why relevant |
|---|---|---|
| *Fantastic Four* (1961 series) #19 (1963) | [Marvel Kang reading list](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/1989/kang-the-conqueror) | Rama-Tut confronts the Fantastic Four, foregrounding Kang's pharaonic time-traveler identity and Marvel's intertwined FF/time-travel storytelling |
| *Avengers* (1963 series) #8 (1964) | [Marvel Kang reading list](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/1989/kang-the-conqueror) | Kang's Avengers debut; context for a modern film originally conceived under the *Kang Dynasty* title |
| *Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars* #1–12 (1984–85) | [Marvel's Kang character history](https://www.marvel.com/characters/kang/in-comics) | Kang and Doom are distinct rival supervillains in the original event; Marvel describes Doom surviving Kang's attack, Kang being killed by Ultron, and Doom later restoring Kang. **Publisher synopsis is the accessible source here, not a full issue reread** |
| *Kang the Conqueror* (2021) #3 (Oct. 13, 2021) | [Marvel official issue page](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/93431/kang_the_conqueror_3) | Explicit Marvel-published synopsis of a Kang-vs-Kang-vs-Doom conflict over history and Doom's timeline |

**Kang/Doom identity caveat:** [Marvel's official Kang character history](https://www.marvel.com/characters/kang/in-comics) describes Rama-Tut's encounter with Doom and an **alleged** ancestry connection. The publisher's biography also depicts many aliases and chronal variants. Do **not** elevate an alleged genealogy to confirmed biological parentage, a fixed multiverse rule, or proof that Doom and Kang are the same person. Likewise, Marvel's issue summaries do not license guesses about unpublished comic plot beats.

## What the film reporting actually establishes

[ComicBook.com, Nov. 16, 2026, quoting Kevin Feige via *Empire*](https://comicbook.com/movies/news/marvels-kevin-feige-reveals-doctor-doom-was-being-planned-as-avengers-5-twist-villain-before-doomsday/) reports **an abandoned development concept**: introducing Doom midway through a film potentially still titled *The Kang Dynasty*. [GamesRadar, Nov. 17](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/marvel-movies/kevin-feige-first-approached-robert-downey-jr-about-returning-as-doctor-doom-at-the-ant-man-3-premiere-months-before-kang-was-scrapped/) reports Feige's **February 2023 outreach** to Robert Downey Jr.'s agent. That reporting is about **production history**, not Marvel's finished 2026 story. See [dated source audit](../../news/2026-11-17.md).

### Hypotheses and evidence boundaries

- **Plausible thematic source (THEORY):** Kang's time-travel technology and Doom's mix of science, sovereignty and magic create an intelligible narrative handoff in a multiversal story. The 2021 Kang #3 and 1984 *Secret Wars* are appropriate **reading comparisons**.
- **Not confirmed:** Doom kills, replaces, absorbs, or descends from Kang in *Doomsday*; a Kang actor appears in the completed film; the 1984 *Secret Wars* deaths/restoration are directly adapted; Rama-Tut's comic family tree is MCU canon; or Marvel filmed any abandoned *Kang Dynasty* script.
- **Distinct issue:** Kevin Feige separately identified Downey's Doom as **Victor von Doom, not a Tony Stark variant** in his **November 12** interview ([original Empire exclusive](https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/avengers-doomsday-doctor-doom-worth-being-scared-of-exclusive/)). Do not confuse a creative handoff between **villains** with an identity reveal between **actors/characters**.
