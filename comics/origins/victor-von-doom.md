# Victor von Doom — origins

Victor is a Latverian Romani scientist, inventor, sorcerer, and monarch. His parents Werner and Cynthia von Doom, family tragedy, intellectual rivalry with Reed Richards, and obsession with controlling his destiny shape his character. Accounts of the accident, scarring, training and accession to power vary between retellings.

## Read these together

- *Fantastic Four* #5 (1962): first published appearance, not the full later origin.
- *Fantastic Four Annual* #2 (1964): foundational origin and Latverian material.
- *Books of Doom* #1–6 (2005–06): retrospective modern retelling.
- *Doctor Strange & Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment* (1989): Cynthia's fate and Doom's magical motivations.

**Movie relevance:** These are thematic possibilities, not confirmation of the film's backstory.
