# Fantastic Four #5 (1962)

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce Doctor Doom in Fantastic Four (1961) #5, cover-dated July 1962. His debut establishes his rivalry with Reed and technological ingenuity, including time travel. Later stories expand his origin.
