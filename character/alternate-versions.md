# Alternate versions and Doombots

Main comic continuity Doom is associated with Earth-616. Multiverse variants and robotic doubles complicate identity. God Emperor Doom is a transformed role during Secret Wars (2015), not automatically a separate person. Do not assume MCU identity from actor casting.
