# Latveria

A fictional European nation, frequently ruled by Doom from its capital Doomstadt. Doom claims his authoritarian rule protects citizens; stories challenge the cost of his control.

## Film marketing evidence — November 6, 2026

**CONFIRMED sponsored marketing, not authenticated film footage:** [Stellantis/Jeep's official November 6 co-branded `Avengers: Doomsday` statement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jeep-brand-takes-adventure-to-marvel-studios-avengers-doomsday-universe-in-epic-finale-of-sweeping-yearlong-global-collaboration-302931380.html) specifically lists **Latveria** as a location visited in its [`A Jeep 4x4 for Any Universe` commercial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uZjSPSuYY7c), calling it **Doctor Doom's nation**. This is the clearest first-party confirmation of that *label in the sponsored ad*. The company's release does **not** certify whether the depicted building/tower is the actual in-film `Doomstadt Tower`, whether Doom's capital is shown in the theatrical feature, or how portals operate. November 5 secondary articles identify a tower amid mountains as a Doomstadt-like structure, **an interpretation beyond the primary release**. No frame-by-frame independent video inspection or exact timestamps were obtained.

**Comics baseline:** [Marvel's `Books of Doom` collection](https://www.marvel.com/comics/collection/124641/doctor_doom_books_of_doom_2025) explicitly follows Victor's path to Latveria's throne; [`One World Under Doom` #1](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/113858/read) presents his expansionist ambitions from Latveria. These are **comic plot facts**, not a movie script. A Latveria visual can ground a Doom-sovereign theory without establishing **Battleworld** or **God Emperor Doom**.
